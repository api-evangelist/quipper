# Quipper

> Quipper is an online learning platform founded in 2010 ("Distributor of Wisdom") that builds web and mobile e-learning products for students and teachers, operating regional education services across Japan, Indonesia, and the Philippines. Quipper is a 500 Global portfolio company. As of this profile no public developer portal or API documentation was found on its public web properties.
